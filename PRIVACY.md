# Sketchtune privacy policy

Sketchtune does not require an account and does not collect personal information to work.

- Drawings and settings are stored on your device.
- If you publish a sketch to the gallery, the drawing, the title and the display name you type are stored on our gallery database and shown publicly.
- If you connect your own AI model, your API key is stored only on your device and requests go directly to the provider you chose.
- The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use the advertising identifier on your device. Purchasing Pro removes ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play; we do not see payment details.
- The app does not read your contacts, location or files beyond the photo you choose to trace, which is processed on your device and never uploaded.
